Every MEXC USDT futures coin, scanned back as far as MEXC keeps candles. An alert is the first moment a coin trades 60% above its lowest price of the previous 24 hours; the "buy" is at that +60% level. A coin can alert again only 24 hours after its last alert. Each alert is then followed for 7 days. Alerts in the last ~30 days are pinned with 1-minute candles; older ones with 5-minute candles (MEXC keeps no older 1-minute data). Coins MEXC has since delisted are not in the scan, so results lean a little optimistic.
